OEM vs ODM underwear: which program fits your brand?

OEM and ODM both put your brand on the product. The difference is where the design and the pattern come from, and that changes how much you need to prepare and how fast you can launch.

Published 2026-11-21

The short answer

Choose ODM if you are launching or testing a line and want proven fits fast. Choose OEM if you already have a design or fit that your customers know, or a product that needs to be different from what is on the market. Many brands start with ODM and move best-sellers to OEM later.

When OEM makes sense

OEM is the right choice when the fit or construction is part of what customers buy from you: a pouch shape, a leg length, a waistband feel. You provide the tech pack and reference samples, and we make the pattern, grade it to your sizes and sample it in the fabric you choose. It takes more development time, but the result is built around your brand.

When ODM makes sense

With ODM you start from one of our developed bases, for example the All seamless bonded boxer brief (M-SB01) or the 60S Lenzing modal brief (ZY05), and change what matters to your customer: fabric, color, waistband, gusset, leg length, labels and packaging. Because the pattern and fabric are already proven in production, there is less risk and less waiting. Our pattern team can still adapt the sizing to your market.

The third option: in-stock with your label

If you need goods quickly or want to test demand, take styles we hold in stock and add your labels and packaging. It is the easiest first order, and the styles that sell can move to an ODM or OEM program later.

Questions to ask any factory

  • Is the quote based on my quantity per style and per color?
  • Which samples are included, and how long does each round take?
  • Is the fabric certified (for example OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100), and can I see the certificate?
  • How is quality inspected, and at which AQL?
  • Who books the freight, and on which term (FOB or CIF)?
